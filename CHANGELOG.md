# Changelog: Ape Escape (Jake Edition Mod)

Changes to the mod itself. `RELEASE_NOTES.md` is the PC recomp's own history.

## v0.10.0

**Jake's races**
- The races are renamed **Spike Attack** (was Stadium Attack) and **Spike Attack 2** (was Gladiator Attack).
- On the race select, Spike's own face replaces the X in "Dimension X" for both races. Other Dimension X levels keep their X.
- Winning now says **"Jake Wins!"**, and losing says "Spike Wins!".
- Your Sky Flyer has Jake's sound, including a matching higher tone for the slow spin. Spike's flyer has Spike's sound.
- Spike's Sky Flyer stays **green**, in his hand and in the air. Yours stays purple.

**Looks**
- **BRAINWASHED** Jake's hair is **blue** on his model and on the HUD. His purple clothes are unchanged.

**Fixes**
- The **Super Hoop**'s spin-and-run sound is back to normal. It played the Sky Flyer's sound in v0.9.0.
- PC version: save states now load straight after restarting the game. Save states made with v0.9.0 won't load in v0.10.0.

## v0.9.0

First release: Jake's model, looks, HUD, purple gadgets and voice. See the README.
